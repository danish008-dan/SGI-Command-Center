Understanding Salary Components

Here is a simple explanation of the standard salary components used in Indian payroll:

  • Basic Salary: The core fixed part of your salary. It is usually 40-50% of the total salary. All other components are often calculated based on this.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): An allowance given to employees to meet the cost of renting a home. It is partially or fully tax-exempt.
  • Special Allowance: A flexible amount given to employees to meet specific needs or to balance the salary structure. It is fully taxable.
  • Provident Fund (PF): A retirement benefit where both employee and employer contribute a percentage of the basic salary (usually 12%). Pending Configuration
  • Professional Tax (PT): A tax levied by state governments in India. It is a small fixed amount deducted monthly. Pending Configuration
  • Tax Deducted at Source (TDS): Income tax deducted from the salary based on the employee's tax slab. Pending Configuration
